Qiskit Fall Fest 2026

Hackathon registration

Join UW Quantum for a free, week-long Qiskit hackathon at the University of Waterloo. The hackathon runs November 1–7, 2026, with Qiskit teaching sessions, technical talks, project support, and team development. Project presentations and judging take place on Friday, November 6, and winners will be announced during the Quantum Symposium on Saturday, November 7.

Please register individually, even if you already have teammates. Teams will form at the beginning of the hackathon. Please submit one response per person using your institutional email where available. All session times are Waterloo local time.

For questions, corrections, or private support requests, contact uwquantumclub@outlook.com.
