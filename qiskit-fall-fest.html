About

Qiskit Fall Fest is a global series of student-run quantum computing events, supported by IBM Quantum. Each fall, campuses around the world run their own chapter, a mix of talks, workshops, and hands-on projects built around Qiskit, IBM's open-source framework for programming real quantum hardware.

This year's theme is IBM Quantum's 10 Years on the Cloud, marking a decade since IBM first put quantum computers on the cloud for anyone to use. The Waterloo Quantum Club is bringing that theme to UW for the first time, looking back at how far quantum computing has come and what's realistic to expect next.

No prior experience is required. The event is designed to take anyone from their first qubit to writing and running real quantum circuits.
